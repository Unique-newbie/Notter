# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**What is NotterPad?**
NotterPad is a privacy-focused, offline-first workspace designed specifically for novelists and creative writers. It combines traditional manuscript drafting with advanced, opt-in AI tools for world-building and lore extraction.

**Is it free?**
Yes, NotterPad is completely free and open-source software.

## Privacy & Storage

**Does it work 100% offline?**
Yes! NotterPad is engineered to function entirely without an internet connection. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use an external API.

**Where is my data stored?**
All your novels, characters, and settings are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. 

**How do I backup my data?**
You can use the built-in export functionality in the Settings to download a complete backup of your workspace, which can be restored on any device.

## AI & Extraction

**Does it write stories for me?**
No. NotterPad is designed to assist writers, not replace them. The AI features are focused on analyzing your existing text to extract lore, generate character profiles, or brainstorm ideas based on your input.

**What AI providers are supported?**
NotterPad utilizes a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) model. We currently support major providers like OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), and Google (Gemini).

**Can I run local LLMs like Ollama?**
Absolutely! You can configure NotterPad to connect to local inference servers like Ollama or LM Studio, ensuring your data never touches an external server, even during AI processing.

## Writing & Features

**Can I export my novels?**
Yes, NotterPad currently supports exporting to standard markdown and text formats. Enhanced export options like EPUB and Docx are planned for future releases.

## Technical & Open Source

**How can I contribute to the project?**
We welcome contributions! Please read our `CONTRIBUTING.md` file in the repository root for guidelines on how to set up the development environment and submit Pull Requests.
